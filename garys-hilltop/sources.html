This is an independent Aether Sites design concept, not an official website for Gary’s Hilltop Auto Repair. Business details were checked September 13, 2026.

Business details

  • Phone and address: the shop’s Facebook page, corroborated by CARFAX.
  • Published services: Yellow Pages service listing and CARFAX service history.
  • Weekday hours: Loc8NearMe, corroborated by the Yellow Pages listing. Weekend listings differ, so please call the shop.

Reviews

The rating, review count and selected excerpts are from CARFAX’s verified service reviews. They are attributed to the reviewers displayed there. Ratings and counts can change.

Photography

The storefront photo was supplied for this project and appears only in the location section. The opening background is a CSS gradient. The supplied SUV photo is not displayed, and the site uses no generated garage photography.

Appointments

The service planner prepares a note in your browser. It sends nothing to the shop, does not store your note on a server, and does not reserve a date. Call the published business number to request an appointment.
