Freshwater

Fish for your freshwater world.

Explore community fish and schooling species with your existing tank in mind.

What to ask ＋

Bring your tank size and a list of its residents. Ask about adult size, group size and temperament before choosing new fish.

Cichlids & angelfish

A little more character.

Discover the cichlid and angelfish side of the shop’s rotating selection.

What to ask ＋

Discuss the exact species, adult space requirements and your current fish. A broad temperament label alone doesn’t establish compatibility.

Plants & shrimp

Make room for the small things.

Live aquatic plants and ornamental shrimp for a closer look at life in the tank.

What to ask ＋

Share your lighting, tank residents and water-test results. Ask which plants and shrimp suit your setup and what’s available today.

Hardscape

Give your layout its shape.

Rocks and driftwood to frame a planted aquarium, from a quiet corner to a full layout.

What to ask ＋

Bring your aquarium’s inside dimensions or a quick sketch. Ask about the material, preparation and suitability for your water conditions.

Saltwater

A different kind of blue.

Marine livestock for saltwater keepers. Ask the shop about its current selection.

What to ask ＋

Have your system size, salinity and existing livestock ready. Confirm the exact species, feeding needs and suitability for your system.

    A little more to know.

    Can I see what’s in stock today?＋

    This page isn’t connected to the shop’s inventory. Call (203) 757-3832 for current species, tank sizes, prices and recent arrivals.

    Can I reserve fish or book online?＋

    This independent concept doesn’t take reservations, payments or bookings. Speak with the shop about any arrangements before visiting.

    What about RO water or saltwater supplies?＋

    Call to confirm whether RO water or premixed saltwater is currently offered, the water type, container requirements and pricing. This page does not confirm a water service or jug size.
