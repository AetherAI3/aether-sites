6 categories to explore

Freshwater fish

Community and schooling fish. Ask which available species suit your aquarium.

What to ask ＋

Bring your tank size and a list of its residents. Ask about adult size, group size and temperament before choosing new fish.

Check current selection ↗

Cichlids & angelfish

Explore the shop’s cichlid and angelfish selection. Plan around the exact species.

What to ask ＋

Discuss the exact species, adult space requirements and your current fish. A broad temperament label alone doesn’t establish compatibility.

Ask about available species ↗

Plants & shrimp

Live aquatic plants and ornamental shrimp from the shop’s dedicated room.

What to ask ＋

Share your lighting, tank residents and water-test results. Ask which plants and shrimp suit your setup and what’s available today.

Build a visit list ↓

Rocks & driftwood

Natural materials for planted layouts, hiding places and a sense of depth.

What to ask ＋

Bring your aquarium’s inside dimensions or a quick sketch. Ask about the material, preparation and suitability for your water conditions.

Plan your layout ↓

Saltwater fish

A selection for marine keepers. Call to discuss your system and what’s available.

What to ask ＋

Have your system size, salinity and existing livestock ready. Confirm the exact species, feeding needs and suitability for your system.

Talk saltwater with the shop ↗

Tanks & supplies

New and used aquariums, equipment and fish food. Check sizes and condition with the shop.

What to ask ＋

Confirm dimensions, condition and what’s included. For filtration and lighting, bring your tank dimensions and the setup you’re planning.
