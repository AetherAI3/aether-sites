This website was created by Aether Sites as an independent design concept for Waterbury Aquarium. It is not the shop’s official website and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the business.

Business information

Address: 406 Watertown Avenue, Waterbury, CT 06708. Phone: (203) 757-3832.

Waterbury Aquarium’s Fishstores.org listing supports the address, phone, freshwater and saltwater categories, plants and ornamental shrimp, hardscape, and new and used aquariums. It lists Monday–Saturday 10 am–8 pm and Sunday 10 am–6 pm. These are directory-listed hours, not owner-confirmed operating hours. Call before visiting.

The site uses broad category descriptions from that listing and the supplied project brief. Specific species, prices, founding dates, availability and services have not been independently confirmed with the owner.

A guide, not live inventory

The catalog filters categories only. It has no inventory feed. Nothing on this page confirms a fish is in stock, compatible with another species or suitable for a particular aquarium. No online purchase, reservation or appointment is accepted.

The aquascape planner generates a checklist locally in your browser. It does not send information to the shop, store it on a server or make a booking. All arrangements happen directly with the business.

Images & design

The planted-aquarium image is an AI-generated illustration, created for this concept using the built-in image-generation tool. It does not depict the store or its current livestock. The glass tank, fish and category illustrations are original CSS/SVG artwork.

The supplied Waterbury Aquarium design kit informed the depth palette, rounded glass, coral buttons and buoyant motion. Motion was adapted for readable layouts, reduced-motion preferences and a static fallback.

What needs owner confirmation

  • Current hours, stock, prices and specific species.
  • Any RO water or premixed saltwater service, sizes and pricing.
  • Booking, reservations, workshops, deliveries and other services.
  • Official logo, real shop photography and any review excerpts.

No fabricated reviews, events or arrivals appear in this concept.
