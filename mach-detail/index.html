BEFORE YOU BOOK

A few
good questions.

Which service should I choose?

Choose a detail for cleaning inside and out. Ask about correction for paint swirls or haze, and ceramic protection for a protective coating. If you are unsure, select “Help choosing a service” and let the shop assess your car.

How much will my detail cost?

Vehicle size, condition, preparation, and the agreed treatment affect the quote. Contact Mach Detail for current pricing before confirming any work.

Does paint correction remove every scratch?

Results depend on paint condition and the depth of the damage. The shop should inspect the finish and explain what can be improved safely before work begins.

Is my appointment confirmed when I use this page?

No. This independent concept helps prepare a request for your messaging app. No live calendar is connected. Your appointment is confirmed only when Mach Detail responds and agrees the details with you.

How long does the work take?

Timing depends on the chosen service, vehicle condition, preparation, and any coating cure requirements. Agree drop-off and collection times with the shop.
