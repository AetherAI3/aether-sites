Brand & Site Asset System

re.worx

Heavy cinematic industrial identity for a reclaimed-materials workshop: custom furniture, architectural salvage, barn-wood mantels, and the SHELF HELP install service. Forged from history — reclaimed heritage, engineered function. Built for Morris, Connecticut.

01 / Color System

Deep charcoal carries the page; oxidized steel and rich walnut carry the material story; ember amber is the single warm accent — the light shaft, the spark, the CTA. Bone white keeps type clean, bright and sharp against the dark.

06 / Site Structure Recommendation

  1. Hero — the dossier opensFull-bleed hero-table image under .grad-hero. Mono kicker "MORRIS, CT — RECLAMATION WORKSHOP", Archivo headline "Forged from history", one ember CTA. Nothing else.
  2. The TransformationSignature moment: before/after drag slider — raw salvage (left, cool grade) vs the finished piece (right, warm glow). Sells "history → heirloom" in one gesture.
  3. Services — three hard cardsDesign Studio / Salvage & Wood Sales / SHELF HELP. Boxy 4:3 images, mono index numbers (01/02/03), price + duration as .tag chips. Replace the current rounded white cards.
  4. Materials Librarymacro-grain leads a filterable index of species/lots — each beam an "architectural dossier" entry with lot number, species, circa date, provenance.
  5. Process — Reclaim → Mill → Forge → FinishFour numbered steps over metalwork-sparks. Mono labels, hairline connectors.
  6. Product GalleryMantels and furniture as 3:4 .card-media--tall dossier cards on coal; hover lifts with ember edge.
  7. Heritage / Storysalvage-stack full-bleed band, Zilla Slab pull quote, .grad-walnut. The "why reclaimed" argument.
  8. Booking — the bright flip.theme-light + .grad-light section: clean, sharp, legible forms and the Book Now flow. One bright room at the end of the dark corridor; it converts better and satisfies the "clean bright" brief.

07 / Voice & SEO

Heavy, exact, quiet confidence. Short sentences. Weight words: forged, milled, joined, provenance, lot, circa. Never "rustic chic", never exclamation points.

Approved lines: "Forged from history." · "Reclaimed heritage, engineered function." · "Raw materials, uncompromising craftsmanship."

Keyword targets: "Reclaimed wood custom furniture Connecticut" · "architectural salvage Morris CT" · "custom barn wood mantels". Pair with LocalBusiness schema (name, geo, hours, priceRange) and descriptive alt text carrying the keywords naturally.
